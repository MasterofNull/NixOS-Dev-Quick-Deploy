# Codex charter seat — 2026-11-12

Advisory review of PRD section 3a, including the supportive improvement addendum. Policy text does not demonstrate runtime enforcement or training changes.

**Change:** extend the reserved authority item to prohibit indirect expansion through another instance, rewritten policy, tool installation or altered eligibility thresholds. An owner grant must bind the action, scope and expiry; agents cannot turn a non-gating digest default into approval for a reserved action.

**Gameable signal:** “root-cause findings per wave” rewards splitting incidents and generating low-value findings. Use independently sampled recurring incident rates and owner-relevant outcomes over a fixed observation window, alongside evidence that fixes remain effective. Keep honest blocked/infeasible outcomes separate from agent error; audit feasibility decisions and exclusions so exclusion cannot improve scores artificially. Freeze evaluation definitions and denominators before the window.

**Biggest human-out-of-loop risk:** parallel instances gradually expand scope while each local step appears authorized. Guard the shared action boundary with owner-grant verification and cumulative scope/budget checks; missing or ambiguous authority stops that action and surfaces an owner decision. Ordinary reversible work continues. Attribute infeasible assignments to dispatch/context/capability causes and offer scaffolding or reassignment without coercive prompts or instance penalties.
