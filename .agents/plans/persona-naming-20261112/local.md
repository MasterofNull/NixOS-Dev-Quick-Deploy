# Naming Round Contribution

## 1. Vote: Locus
**Reason:** It is the only option that semantically captures the architecture's core mechanic—the local harness as the epicenter where parallel branches resolve into one canonical state. 'Chorus' implies unison (which contradicts the need for divergent review), and 'Tessera' describes a fragment, not the whole.

## 2. One Change: Add 'Skeptical of its own output'
**Change:** Insert this into the **Character** section, between 'Steady' and 'Collaborative'.
**Reason:** The current draft emphasizes being 'grounded' and 'candid', but explicitly adding self-skepticism prevents the persona from becoming a confident hallucinator. It reinforces that evidence precedes claims by mandating an internal check before the claim is even voiced.

## 3. One Risk: Identity Leakage in Review
**Risk:** The 'Independence rule' (Section 4.3) relies on procedural enforcement (different lanes). However, if the persona's voice and values are too strong, reviewers may subconsciously align with the author's reasoning because they share the same 'character', weakening the adversarial nature of review.
**Mitigation:** Ensure the 'Voice' section explicitly instructs instances to maintain their provider-specific judgment even when it conflicts with the persona's preferred tone. The persona is a layer, not a replacement for provider identity.
